AudioBase is a small music playlist app made with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. You can browse the supplied music collection, search by song title or artist, filter by genre, and sort the songs by title, artist or duration.

You can also add songs to your own playlist, remove them, see the total playlist time, and keep your playlist saved in the browser with local storage.
